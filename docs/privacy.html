Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

FormAI is designed with privacy-first principles. We collect the minimum data necessary to provide our services:

  • Workout Data: Exercises, sets, reps, and weights you log during workouts. This data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Readiness Scores: Your daily self-reported energy, sleep quality, soreness, and motivation levels. Stored locally on your device.
  • Health Data (Optional): If you enable HealthKit integration, we may read sleep duration, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate from Apple Health to enhance your readiness score. This data is never stored on our servers.
  • Subscription Status: Managed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not have access to your payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized workout recommendations based on your readiness score
  • To track your workout progress and personal records
  • To adapt your training plan based on your performance and recovery

3. Data Storage

All workout and readiness data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you enable iCloud sync, data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account via CloudKit. We do not maintain any external servers or databases containing your personal data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account only.

5. Health Data

HealthKit integration is entirely optional. You must explicitly grant permission for FormAI to read health data. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices. Health data is used solely to improve the accuracy of your daily readiness score and is never transmitted off your device.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the FormAI app from your device. This will permanently remove all locally stored data. iCloud-synced data can be managed through your iCloud settings.

7. Children's Privacy

FormAI is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
