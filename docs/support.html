Managing Your Subscription

How to Cancel

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone/iPad
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Find "FormAI" and tap it
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

Restore Purchases

If you've purchased FormAI Pro but don't see your features:

  1. Open FormAI
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap "Restore Purchases"

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Readiness Score?

The Readiness Score is a 0-100 value calculated from your self-reported energy, sleep quality, soreness, and motivation. It determines how your workout will be adjusted for the day.

How does the AI adapt my workout?

Based on your Readiness Score, FormAI adjusts your training volume, intensity, and RPE targets. Higher scores may lead to increased volume, while lower scores trigger scaled-down or deload sessions.

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud. We never share your data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
