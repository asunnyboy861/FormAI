## Promotional Text
Your body changes daily. Your workouts should too. FormAI adapts every session to your readiness score.

## Description
FormAI is the first workout app that adapts to how you feel today, not just what you did yesterday.

Most fitness apps give you the same plan regardless of whether you slept well, feel sore, or are low on energy. FormAI is different. Every morning, you answer four quick questions about your energy, sleep, soreness, and motivation. Our Readiness Engine then adjusts your workout in real time - scaling volume, swapping exercises, and modifying intensity so you always train at the right level.

No more forcing through heavy squats on three hours of sleep. No more skipping the gym because your legs are still wrecked. FormAI meets you where you are, every single day.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Check In (30 seconds)
Rate your energy, sleep quality, soreness, and motivation on a simple 1-5 scale. FormAI calculates your Readiness Score instantly.

2. Get Your Adaptive Workout
Based on your score, FormAI adjusts your plan automatically:
- High readiness? Push for PRs and add volume.
- Medium readiness? Focus on technique with moderate loads.
- Low readiness? Switch to recovery-focused movements and reduce intensity.

3. See Why Every Adjustment Was Made
Unlike black-box AI, FormAI shows you exactly why each change was made. Transparency builds trust.

4. Track Progress Over Time
View weekly volume trends, readiness patterns, and personal records. See how your body responds to training and recovery.

KEY FEATURES

- Daily Readiness Score: 4-dimension check-in (energy, sleep, soreness, motivation) drives every workout adjustment

- Smart Progressive Overload: Safe, science-based weight and volume increases

- Data Export: Download your workout history in CSV or JSON format
